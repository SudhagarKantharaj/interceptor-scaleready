# 5–7 minute demo script

## 1. Introduce the problem (45 seconds)

“Interceptor ScaleReady explores how a river-cleanup programme could connect evidence, readiness questions, dependencies and follow-up actions. It is a research prototype, not an official tool or an assessment of any real deployment.”

## 2. Show research and evidence (60 seconds)

Open Research overview, then Evidence register. Explain that findings should be traceable to source material and that source presence alone does not prove every possible claim.

## 3. Show readiness and dependencies (60 seconds)

Open Deployment Dependencies and Readiness Profile. Explain that “Not assessed” means there is insufficient assessment recorded; it does not mean a real-world gap exists.

## 4. Show action ownership (60 seconds)

Open Actions & Handover. Use a clearly labelled fictional example such as “Confirm waste-transfer arrangements.” Show its owner, target date, linked readiness area/evidence if valid, and open status.

## 5. Show the integrated report (60 seconds)

Open Integrated Readiness Report. Explain how it consolidates existing records and offers report/export options without re-entering the same information.

## 6. Show persistence and export (45 seconds)

Refresh the app, verify the test action remains, then open a downloaded CSV or text report.

## 7. Close with validation questions (30 seconds)

Ask whether this workflow maps to how a project team currently makes readiness decisions, what systems already support it, and whether any step would materially reduce coordination effort.

## Demo safety

Label all invented records as illustrative. Never imply the prototype has been used by The Ocean Cleanup or that it has uncovered verified operational shortcomings.
