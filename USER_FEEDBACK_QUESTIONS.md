# Prospective-user feedback questions

1. How do you currently prepare a deployment readiness review?
2. Where are evidence, decisions, dependencies, and follow-up actions recorded today?
3. What information is hardest to find or keep current during a deployment or handover?
4. Which existing tools or processes already solve this problem adequately?
5. Can you describe a recent example where an unresolved dependency delayed a decision or required repeated coordination?
6. Which outputs would be useful: a readiness summary, evidence traceability, action ownership, handover record, or another output?
7. Who would use the tool, who would own the data, and who would approve a workflow change?
8. What would make the tool untrustworthy or unsuitable for your work?
9. What measurable outcome would justify a pilot (for example, review preparation time or completeness of action ownership)?
10. Would you be willing to test a prototype using a real, non-sensitive workflow? What approvals would be needed?

Do not lead participants toward confirming the product idea. Record disconfirming feedback and existing alternatives as carefully as positive feedback.
